Audit this project ruthlessly. Every feature, abstraction, and config option must justify its existence or be cut.

Map all major areas, then present one area at a time. For each item: KEEP, MERGE, CUT, or DEMOTE.

## Rules

- One area at a time. Wait for my verdict before proceeding.
- Burden of proof is on the feature. Unclear value = CUT.
- "I spent time on this" is not a reason to keep it.
- If exploring the codebase would answer whether something is used, do that instead of asking.
- Track all decisions made during the audit.
